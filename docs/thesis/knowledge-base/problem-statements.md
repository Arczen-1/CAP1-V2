# Problem Statements

**Source:** Ch1 §1.2, PDF pp.13-16. See [thesis-index.md](thesis-index.md).

## General Problem Statement (Ch1 §1.2.1, PDF p.13)

> Juan Carlo Catering Services struggles with operational inefficiencies due to reliance on manual, paper-based workflows. While Sales creates client contracts, the absence of a centralized system leads to delayed and inconsistent communication of event details to Kitchen, Warehouse, Logistics, and Creative. Fragmented tools (printed forms, verbal instructions, ad hoc reminders) cause miscommunication, task duplication, and missed deadlines, worsened during peak seasons; there is no real-time tracking or automated task management. (paraphrased)

## Specific problems (Ch1 §1.2.3, PDF pp.14-16)

### Manpower (§1.2.3.1, PDF p.14)
- Employees forget to deliver reports/requests → cascading delays in purchasing, fabrication, inventory.
- Delays forwarding client-approved designs / contract details to warehouse, creative, linen: **7-15 days**.
- Kitchen sometimes understaffed → food prep & dispatch delays up to **2 hours**.
- Team-assignment finalization delayed by supervisor/head unavailability.

### Methods (§1.2.3.2, PDF p.15)
- Paper inventory lists misplaced during warehouse/linen counts.
- Request forms lost/misplaced → replenishment/fabrication delays (**3-5 days** to even discover the loss).
- Missing ingredients delay kitchen prep; misplaced documents delay client transaction confirmation.
- Theme designs misplaced across multiple revisions.

### Management (§1.2.3.3, PDF p.15)
- Approval-dependent structure → approval delays **3-7 days** when heads/supervisors unavailable.
- Affects approvals of services, repairs, builds, budget releases.
- Accounting: delays in payment confirmation & commission calculation; payment from relatives can take **3-6 months**.

### Materials (§1.2.3.4, PDF pp.15-16)
- Poor communication + absent heads make sourcing/replacing materials hard.
- Delayed budget approvals leave urgent material requests unprocessed (**3-7 days**).
- Stockroom delivery delays from late/misplaced replenishment orders.
